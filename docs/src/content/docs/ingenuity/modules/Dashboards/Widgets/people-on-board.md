The POB widget stands for People on Board. This widget is primarily used to monitor who is offshore.

Depending on the datasource configured the POB widget may show: number of people on board, details about the people on board such as name, role.

To add to your dashboard, drag and drop from the widget menu. 

![POB Widget](~/assets/dashboards/image175.png)

Connect the datasource previously configured for the POB.
