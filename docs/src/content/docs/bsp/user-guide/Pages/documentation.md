The Documentation page provides a centralised library of documents associated with the barrier model and the broader safety management framework of the facility. Having documents accessible directly within the BSP means that users can move from reviewing a barrier status to accessing the relevant procedure, standard, or guidance document without leaving the system.

![Visual Asset Diagram](~/assets/bsp/components/image33.png)

Not all BSP deployments include a dedicated documentation page. The documents stored here, and how they are organised, will depend on your organisation's requirements and what has been configured during deployment.

##	What documents are stored here?

The specific documents available will vary by organisation, but the documentation page typically contains relevant documents to the BSP such as: Barrier management and performance standards, physical security documents, training documents, management manuals etc..
