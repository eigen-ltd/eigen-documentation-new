View all Major Accident Hazards that are relevant to your facility/asset. Hazards will typically be grouped by area categories that they affect, for example: categories could be Asset Areas and Subsea Areas. Under each Hazard different work orders will be listed.

![Status Tables](~/assets/bsp/components/image29.png)

##	Navigation

Drill down to Individual Major Accident Hazards by selecting one from the landing MAH page.

![Visual Asset Diagram](~/assets/bsp/components/image30.png)

You can then navigate between hazards.
