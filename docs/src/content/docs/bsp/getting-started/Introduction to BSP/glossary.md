---
title: 'Glossary'
description: Definitions of key terms used throughout BSP, including BSP, MAH, barrier functions, performance standards, signals, impairments, and scores.

  order: 5
---

**BSP** - Barrier Status Panel. The application described in this manual. 
 
**Barrier Strategy Area / Protected Area** - A logically defined area of a facility for the purposes of barrier management. This may differ from a physical area, as the equipment protecting an area is not always located within it. 
 
**Barrier Function** - A function implemented to prevent or mitigate a major accident hazard. Also referred to as a Safety and Environmentally Critical Barrier (SECB). Barrier functions are implemented through physical barrier elements. 
 
**Barrier Element** - The physical or logical equipment that implements a barrier function, such as a sensor, valve, or trip system. Also referred to as Safety and Environmentally Critical Elements (SECEs). 
 
**Operational Barrier** - A procedural barrier, such as competency requirements, risk assessments, or control of work processes.

**Performance Standard** - An industry-recognised standard that a barrier must adhere to, typically grouped by category such as fire and gas detection, process control, or emergency escape. 
 
**Major Accident Hazard (MAH)** — A significant hazard identified through a formal hazard study, such as a fire, explosion, gas release, oil spill, or collision. Barriers are typically traceable back to one or more MAHs. 
 
**Bow-tie Diagram** - A visual representation of the relationship between hazard causes, the central hazard event, and its consequences, with barriers shown on either side. Used to structure the barrier model. 
 
**BSP Signal** - A standardised message produced by a data connector, containing information about the state of a piece of equipment or element. Signals are the unit of information processed by the business rules engine. 
 
**Impairment** - A condition in which a barrier or barrier element is degraded or unavailable, reducing its ability to perform its protective function. 
 
**Score** - A numerical value assigned to a barrier or element based on the type and criticality of impairments present, used to calculate status.
